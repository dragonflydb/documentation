## Overview

Dragonfly Cloud bills on a usage basis. You pay only for what you use, prorated hourly,
and invoices are generated monthly.

## Where to Find Your Usage

Track current usage and costs under the [Account > Usage](https://dragonflydb.cloud/account/usage) tab
in [Dragonfly Cloud](https://dragonflydb.cloud/).
The usage and cost information is updated every few hours.
However, data transfer may take up to 24 hours to reflect.
